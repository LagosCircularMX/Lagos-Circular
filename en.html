Alignment with Jalisco's Circular Economy Law

Our project complies with the principles and provisions of the law published in September 2025

ArticleProvisionHow LAGOS CIRCULAR complies
Article 4Eco-design, waste valorization, green jobsProducts are designed from plastic and organic waste; 25 direct green jobs are created.
Article 12Municipal Waste Management PlansIt offers a Management Plan model ready to be replicated in other municipalities.
Article 18Shared responsibility among government, business and citizensIntegrates citizens through 5 community collection centers operated by local cooperatives.
Article 22Promotion of investment in valorization infrastructureThe project is eligible for the Green Financing Program (FOJAL) and seeks complementary private investment.

LAGOS CIRCULAR is designed to be the first demonstrative case of this law in the Los Altos region.

SWOT Analysis

Strategic diagnosis for decision making

Strengths

  • ✅ Local raw material at almost zero cost
  • ✅ Patentable thermofusion and molding technology
  • ✅ Recognized by Fundación POSiBLE 2026
  • ✅ Strategic alliance with ENERWASTE
  • ✅ Alignment with the Circular Economy Law

Weaknesses

  • ⚠️ High initial investment ($2.4M MXN)
  • ⚠️ Dependence on municipal agreement

Opportunities

Frequently Asked Questions for Investors

Technical and financial answers for strategic decision-making

What is the maximum production capacity and how will it scale?

Phase 1: 200 kg/h, 1,600 kg/day, up to 34,000 bricks per day.

How is structural stability guaranteed on a landfill?

Soil mechanics study ($45,000 MXN), reinforced concrete platform ($425,000 MXN), and biogas ventilation system.

What is the environmental impact of plastic washing?

Closed-loop water recirculation system. 95% reuse. Zero discharges to the municipal sewer.

What is the plant's energy demand?

77 kW total. 100 kVA substation. Estimated monthly cost: $22,000 MXN.

What additives ensure the durability of livestock mats?

Recycled LDPE, thermoplastic elastomer, foaming agent, antimicrobial agent, and UV stabilizer.

What is the NPV and IRR of the project?

NPV: +$1,200,000 MXN. IRR: 62% per year. Discounted payback: 2.6 years.

How is intellectual property protected?

Provisional patent before IMPI, trademark registration, copyright, and signed NDAs.

Why should ENERWASTE invest in LAGOS CIRCULAR?

Already developed technology, immediate synergy, no opportunity cost, and a projected return of $787,500 MXN annually from Year 3.
